Ghana context
At $0.86/L, Ghana is competitive regionally — cheaper than Côte d'Ivoire, Burkina Faso and Senegal. However Ghana's fuel is fully market-priced with no subsidy, meaning every global price shock passes through directly to consumers and drivers. Nigeria benefits from Dangote Refinery reducing import dependency. Cedi update: $1 = GHS 11.00 (strengthened from ~15.5 earlier in 2026), which should ease future import cost pressures on pump prices.
Fuel price increment history — Ghana 2020 to Apr 2026
Apr 2026 · Window 1
NPA raises floor amid Hormuz crisis
Global crude surged +55% in March 2026 — record since 1988. NPA forced to raise price floor sharply. Competition among OMCs intensifies: Star Oil and GOIL match the floor exactly. TotalEnergies prices GHS 1.19 above the minimum.
Petrol: GHS 13.30/LDiesel: GHS 17.10/L+15% petrol MoM
Mar 2026 · Window 2 (Mar 16–31)
Sharp mid-March increase
Hormuz Strait transit collapses to near zero from Feb 28 following US-Iran conflict. Global crude shock begins propagating to retail pump prices. COMAC warns of 16–17% projected increases for April.
Petrol: GHS 11.57/LDiesel: GHS 14.35/L
Jan 2026 · Window 1 (Jan 1–15)
Significant price relief — cedi strengthens
Cedi appreciated 8.2% vs USD (GHS 11.14→10.50). Global crude fell. Star Oil led cuts to GHS 10.56/L — lowest price in years. GOIL followed with GHS 10.99, later GHS 9.99 at 150 selected stations in Window 2. COPEC urged OMCs and ride-hailing platforms to pass savings to consumers.
Petrol: GHS 10.56–10.99/LDiesel: GHS 11.56–11.96/LBest price since 2023
Jul 2025
D-Levy enforcement — GHS 1.00 energy sector levy
Ghana Revenue Authority enforces Energy Sector Levies (Amendment) Act 1141. GHS 1.00/L surcharge on petrol and diesel to address energy sector debt exceeding $3 billion. Prices spike ~14.6% from previous window. GOIL: petrol GHS 12.88/L, diesel GHS 14.38/L. Star Oil: petrol GHS 12.77/L.
Petrol: GHS 12.77–12.88/LDiesel: GHS 13.45–14.38/L+GHS 1.00 D-Levy
Jan–Feb 2025
Moderate relief as global crude eases
GOIL petrol drops from GHS 14.99 to GHS 12.28. TotalEnergies and Shell cut by ~GHS 3.80/L. Cedi showed some resilience early 2025. COPEC welcomed reductions but noted not all OMCs passed on savings immediately due to expensive stock.
Petrol: GHS 12.28/L (GOIL)Diesel: GHS 12.77/L
2024
Sustained high prices — cedi depreciation 40.88%
Cedi depreciated 40.88% against USD in 2024 — worst year on record. Petrol and diesel averaged 10% higher than 2023. Prices stayed above GHS 13/L for most of year. VIT (Vehicle Income Tax) introduced in January 2024 for ride-hailing vehicles. GOIL: petrol GHS 14.42/L (Aug 2024).
Petrol avg: ~GHS 13–14.50/LDiesel avg: ~GHS 14–15/LCedi −40.88% vs USD
Apr 2023
Digital Transport Fee introduced — GHS 1.00/trip
DTF GHS 1.00 per completed trip introduced for all ride-hailing platforms. Uber raises minimum fare GHS 7→8 (+14%), cuts driver commission 25%→20% in compensation. Fuel at ~GHS 10–11/L following post-2022 correction.
Petrol: ~GHS 10.50/LDTF: GHS 1.00/trip added
Nov 2022 — All-time high
Petrol peaks at GHS 18.85/L — all-time record
Global energy crisis + Russia-Ukraine war + G7 oil price cap + cedi depreciation of 40%+ in 2022. Ghana enters debt crisis. Petrol hits all-time high GHS 18.85/L. Diesel follows at similar levels. Severe economic hardship for drivers and households.
Petrol: GHS 18.85/L ← all-time highCedi: −40%+ vs USD in 2022
2021
Post-COVID recovery — gradual price rise
Global crude recovers from COVID lows. Supply chain disruptions and rising freight costs push prices up. Petrol climbs from below GHS 5/L to ~GHS 5.50/L. Ride-hailing demand recovers as mobility returns.
Petrol: ~GHS 5.50/LDiesel: ~GHS 5.20/L
2020 — Baseline
COVID lows — petrol below GHS 5/L
COVID-19 lockdowns collapse global oil demand. Crude prices historic low. Ghana pump prices subdued. Petrol stays below GHS 5/L. This is the baseline for all subsequent % comparisons.

Methodology & update schedule
NPA fuel floors
Updated every 2 weeks (biweekly NPA pricing windows). Floors announced by NPA; actual pump prices set by OMCs at or above the floor.
Station prices
Sourced from CitiNewsroom market checks, COPEC surveys, and OMC press releases. Represent Accra area stations only. Prices valid for the stated window only.
West Africa table
USD/L for cross-country comparability. Converted from local currencies at prevailing rates. Updated monthly or on major price events.
Platform fare rates
Entered manually in the Fare Structure tab. Based on operator knowledge and public announcements. Uber base fare GHS 8.00 confirmed; per-km and per-min rates require verification from current app pricing.
Trip cost estimates
Calculated as: base fare + (per km × distance) + (per min × time) + (wait rate × wait time). Booking fee applied on subtotal. Platform fee added after. Estimates assume standard non-surge conditions.
Driver economics chart
Fuel cost = 200 km/day × 10L/100km × NPA floor diesel price. Earnings = estimated based on industry avg GHS 30/trip × assumed trips/day. Indicative only — actual earnings vary widely.
Data storage
All user edits (fare rates, situation report, initiatives) saved to browser localStorage. Data persists on page reload. Clearing browser storage resets to defaults.
