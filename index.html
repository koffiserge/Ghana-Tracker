Fuel price increment history — Ghana 2020 to Apr 2026
Apr 2026 · Window 1
NPA raises floor amid Hormuz crisis
Global crude +55% in March 2026 — record since 1988. GOIL and Star Oil match NPA floor exactly. TotalEnergies prices GHS 1.19 above minimum.
Petrol: GHS 13.30/LDiesel: GHS 17.10/L+15% petrol MoM
Mar 2026 · Window 2
Sharp mid-March increase
Hormuz Strait transit collapses to near zero from Feb 28 following US-Iran conflict. Global supply shock begins propagating to retail pump prices.
Petrol: GHS 11.57/LDiesel: GHS 14.35/L
Jan 2026 · Window 1
Significant relief — cedi strengthens
Cedi appreciated 8.2% vs USD. Star Oil led cuts to GHS 10.56/L — lowest in years. GOIL followed at GHS 9.99 at 150 selected stations. COPEC urged ride-hailing platforms to pass savings to consumers.
Petrol: GHS 10.56–10.99/LBest price since 2023
Jul 2025
D-Levy enforcement — GHS 1.00 energy levy
Energy Sector Levies Act 1141 enforced. GHS 1.00/L on petrol and diesel. Prices spike ~14.6%. GOIL: petrol GHS 12.88/L, diesel GHS 14.38/L.
Petrol: GHS 12.77–12.88/L+GHS 1.00 D-Levy
Jan–Feb 2025
Moderate relief
GOIL petrol drops from GHS 14.99 to GHS 12.28. TotalEnergies and Shell cut by ~GHS 3.80/L. Cedi showed resilience early 2025.
Petrol: GHS 12.28/LDiesel: GHS 12.77/L
2024
Sustained high prices — cedi −40.88%
Cedi depreciated 40.88% vs USD in 2024. VIT (Vehicle Income Tax) introduced Jan 2024 for ride-hailing. GOIL petrol peaked at GHS 14.42/L (Aug 2024).
Petrol avg: GHS 13–14.50/LCedi −40.88%
Apr 2023
Digital Transport Fee — GHS 1.00/trip
DTF GHS 1.00/trip introduced. Uber raises min fare GHS 7→8, cuts commission 25%→20%.
Petrol: ~GHS 10.50/LDTF: GHS 1.00/trip

Methodology
NPA fuel floors
Updated every 2 weeks (biweekly NPA pricing windows). Actual pump prices set by OMCs at or above the floor.
Trip cost estimates
Base fare + (per km × 10) + (per min × 15) + (wait × 2). Service fee on subtotal. Platform fee added after.
Driver economics
Fuel cost = 200 km/day × 10L/100km × NPA diesel floor. Earnings = indicative avg GHS 30/trip.
Data storage
All edits saved to browser localStorage and persist on reload.
